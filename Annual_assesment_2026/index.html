Finding supernovae in the LSST data stream

  • Vera Rubin / LSST: one image every 60 s for 10 years, ~10 k differences per image.
  • Each difference → Real/Bogus classification: true transient or detector artifact?
  • No ground truth, only weak, noisy labels → a weakly-supervised problem.

Robust training + calibrated uncertainties

Methods

  • Asymmetric Co-Teaching: learns under label noise while protecting the high-stakes class.
  • The two co-trained models reused as an ensemble (+ MC dropout) for uncertainty.

Results

  • Best calibration at ~25× less compute than deep ensembles.
  • Uncertainties correlate with physics (SNR, brightness); UMAP confirms global behavior.

Passed first round of review for Astronomy & Astrophysics.

Latent space, interactive UMAP

Multiclass classification without labels

  • Extends binary CWoLa to \(K>2\) classes: train one classifier on mixture identity → the posterior forms a \((K-1)\)-simplex whose vertices are the classes.
  • No labels, no priors; also recovers the mixing matrix and the number of classes \(K\). Validated on CIFAR-10 and Galaxy10.

Submitted to A* CS conference.

The geometry is really there

CIFAR-10, \(K=3\): trained only on mixture id, points cluster at vertices matching the oracle classes, via post-hoc fit (a) or bottleneck (b).

Feature attribution for proton structure: Shapley meets NNPDF

  • PDFs = the latent model inferred from LHC data.
  • Only 8 flavors → \(2^7=128\) coalitions → the exact Shapley value (no independence assumption, correlations kept).
  • Attributes the \(\chi^2\) contribution per flavor and per dataset, in any kinematic region.

To be submit to top-tier Physics Journal.

Mechanistic interpretability for a neutrino foundation model

  • PolarBERT: foundation model trained on 131M IceCube events.
  • A Sparse Autoencoder on its representation exposes interpretable latent features (clean / auxiliary / charge axes).
  • Key finding: representation ≠ causation — readable features are not necessarily the ones the model uses.
